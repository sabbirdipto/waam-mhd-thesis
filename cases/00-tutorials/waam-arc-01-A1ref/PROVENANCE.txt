This is the 1512 W COUNTERPART to waam-arc-01-0p5mm-790W. Same mesh, same
arc_x0_m = -0.010, same inletOutlet top boundary, same DECOMP (6,1,1).
The only difference is arc_power_W. Use the pair for the heat-input comparison.

arc_power_W 1512.0 = eta * U * I with eta = 0.70 taken as the POOL-ONLY
efficiency. Zhu et al. (Int J Heat Mass Transf 126:1206-1221) Eq. 8 defines
eta as the TOTAL: eta_1 = eta - eta_2, eta_2 = 0.334 for these parameters.
With droplets separately entering as mass at 1506 K (722 W), this delivers
2234 W against U*I = 2160 W -- 103%, impossible. Correct value is 790 W.
